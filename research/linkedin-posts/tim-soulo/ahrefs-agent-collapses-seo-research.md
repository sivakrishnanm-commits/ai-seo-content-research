# My mind is blown.

My mind is blown.

I just spent time inside Ahrefs Agent A and the way SEOs and agencies operate is about to fundamentally change. 🤯

I asked it to pull the backlinks our competitors have that we don't. Then I asked it to surface outreach opportunities based on those gaps. Real Ahrefs data. Direct. Conversational. No clicking through 14 reports. No exporting CSVs. No filtering, sorting, and pivoting for an hour to get to one insight.

Just answers.

Here's what hit me:

The hours marketers used to burn doing this work — gone. The clicks, the exports, the manual cross-referencing — gone. What's left is the part that actually matters. The thinking. The strategy. The creative leverage. The stuff that makes marketing special in the first place.

Two kinds of agencies are about to exist:
1. Pre-agent agencies. Still selling hours. Still clicking buttons. Still operating on yesterday's playbook.
2. Post-agent agencies. Using agents as teammates. Freeing humans to do work only humans can do.

I've been early to a few things in my career. Reddit for B2B back in 2018. GEO in 2024. This one feels bigger than both.

If you're a marketer reading this and you haven't put your hands on an agent yet… close LinkedIn and go try one today. The gap between agent-native marketers and everyone else is going to widen fast.

The craft is changing. Embrace it or get left behind.
